1. ​Clean the metal: Wipe down every aluminum piece with rubbing alcohol to remove any oils or factory residue.
​Scuff the surface: Lightly rub the pieces with fine-grit sandpaper or a Scotch-Brite pad so the primer has something to grip onto. Wipe them clean again.
​Apply primer: Spray a light, even coat of metal self-etching primer. Let it dry completely. This keeps the paint from peeling or flaking off the metal.
​Spray the color: Apply your neon green and hot pink car-grade paint in several thin, light coats instead of one heavy coat. This stops it from running or dripping. Let it dry between coats.
​Seal with clear coat: Once the color paint is completely dry, finish it with a clear coat (an automotive or 2K clear coat is best) to give it a tough, glossy shell that resists scratches.
​Let it cure: Give all the painted pieces a full 24 to 48 hours to completely harden before you start building and bolting the robot together.
