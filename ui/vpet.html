Make a folder in ui/vpet/ — the folder is the creature's name — and put its states in it. The filename is the state.
  • orc/idle.png — what he does most of the time
  • orc/walk.png — wandering
  • orc/happy.png — after you play with him
  • orc/sad.png — poked one too many times
  • orc/sleep.png — napping
  • bg.jpg — the room, shared by everyone
A sprite strip works as-is: a 600×100 PNG is six frames. Missing states fall back to whatever exists, so one idle.png is enough to start, and a second folder turns the name button on.
