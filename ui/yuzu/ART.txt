YUZU — where her pictures came from
===================================

  zebra.png   220x551 RGBA, transparent background.
  cream.png   220x551 RGBA, transparent background.

              Both were cut out of ONE image Ghost sent on Sept 11
              2026 (420x594 RGBA) showing the same character twice,
              side by side, in two winter outfits:

                zebra   dark fur-trimmed jacket over a zebra-print
                        cardigan, black skirt, grey fluffy leg
                        warmers and boots, grey tail
                cream   white beret with a pompom, cream poncho,
                        white shorts, white knee socks, white tail

PROVENANCE IS NOT RECORDED, same as Cait's picture and for the same
reason: it arrived as a finished image and nobody wrote down where it
came from at the time. Saya's faces are Ghost's OWN drawings (ui/raw/
holds the originals off his phone). These are not known to be.

Being straight about it, once, in the place it matters: this is a
personal build on his own hardware, which is fine. If the deck is ever
shown off publicly, credit these or swap them.

HOW THEY WERE SPLIT, and why it is not a straight cut
-----------------------------------------------------
The two figures OVERLAP in x (210..220): the right one's raised fist
crosses into the left one's column. A vertical cut at the thinnest
column left a scrap of her hand floating beside the wrong girl.

They are two connected components, though, and nothing else in the
image is. So each figure is masked by its own component, the mask is
dilated 2px to put the anti-aliased fringe back (a bare component mask
at alpha>=32 shaves the soft edge and leaves a hard one), and the
original pixels are kept inside it.

ONE CANVAS ACROSS BOTH, NEVER ONE PER OUTFIT. Each is pasted at the
same offset from her HEAD CENTRE and her top of head, into a canvas
sized by the union of the two. That is why she does not jump, grow or
shuffle sideways when the outfit changes -- the same rule the V-Pet's
sprites follow ("ONE box across every state of a character, never one
per state, or he changes size when his mood does"). Verified by
compositing the two on top of each other and looking: head, shoulders
and feet land in the same place.

ADDING A THIRD OUTFIT
---------------------
Drop a PNG in this folder. That is the whole procedure -- yuzu_face.
outfits() lists the folder, /outfits.json is regenerated per request,
and the filename is the name on the button. No list in the page, no
list in her prompt, no server restart.

Match this canvas (220x551) and register her head to the same place,
or she will jump when he switches to it.
