zero.jpg -- Ghost's pick for the brainy character, Sept 24.

A screenshot off his phone, 735x1086. The phone's own buttons were
painted into it: a round BACK arrow top-left and a LENS icon
bottom-right. Cropped out rather than painted over -- x 96..735,
y 0..972 -- so the art is untouched, only framed tighter. Losing the
lower ribs and a strip of wall costs nothing on a 1024x600 panel,
where she is shown full height on the left.

Not cut out, and not meant to be: it is a whole illustration with its
own background, shown as a card on a black page (his call: "Black
background").

THE BLUE WAS TAKEN OUT, Sept 24. Ghost: "Can you make her background
more black? Blues not my vibe rly". Done in the same single pass as
the crop, from the original screenshot, so the art is re-encoded
once. Every pixel whose hue is blue through violet to magenta
(roughly 165..335 degrees, strongest at 250) loses its colour, and
how far it DARKENS depends on how light it was:

    dark blue   (the machine room)          -> sinks toward black
    light blue  (the shading on her plating) -> just turns grey

The second half matters: darkening everything blue by the same
amount left dark blotches on her shoulders and chest, which read as
bruises. Reds, the bone of her plating and the lamp are untouched,
because none of them are blue. Done with headless Chromium (no PIL
in the container); rendered side by side with the original and
looked at before it shipped.
