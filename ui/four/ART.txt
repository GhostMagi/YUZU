FOUR — one picture, and the one character whose art is NOT cut out.

source    bdecaf879c2e9278a374af2f7be206db.jpg, dropped in the repo root
          by Ghost on Sept 15 and moved here. 736x1104, 114KB, untouched.
          An ASCII/matrix portrait: a girl rendered in green characters
          on black, one eye lit.

cut       NONE. Do not run yuzu_cutout.py over this file.

WHY NOT, measured on the source before the decision:

    border brightness   median 1.0 of 255   (the backdrop is pure black)
    near-black pixels   55.8% of the whole picture

The second number is the one that decides it. Her shadow side IS the
backdrop colour, and unlike `ghost_crowd` there is no outline between
them -- she fades continuously into it. ART.txt's own rule applies with
nothing to get around it: a flood from the edge walks straight into her
face and takes half of it, at any tolerance that clears the backdrop at
all.

AND SHE DOES NOT NEED CUTTING. She is BRIGHT ON BLACK and her page is
black, so `mix-blend-mode: screen` in ui/four.html composites her
exactly: black contributes nothing, the backdrop disappears with every
soft edge intact, and the code rain behind her shows through her dark
side. That is the animation, not a workaround -- she is made of running
characters, so the characters run.

The general note, worth having next to the cutout tool: a backdrop that
matches the PAGE does not have to be removed at all. The tool exists to
get rid of a backdrop that clashes. Check which one you have first.

NOT resized. 114KB is already smaller than any of Mimi's six, and she
renders about 560px tall on the panel against a 1104px source, so there
is headroom rather than waste.
