WHAT LIVES IN THIS FOLDER
=========================

THE FILENAME IS THE STATE. Drop files in, refresh the page, done.
There is no list to edit and no build step.

RIGHT NOW

    idle.png    6 cels    what he does most of the time
    walk.png    8 cels    wandering across the room
    happy.png   7 cels    playing -- he swings the blade
    sad.png     4 cels    poked one too many times
    sleep.png   1 cel     napping
    bg.jpg                the room

All of it is Demon_A from the free Tiny RPG pack 02, cropped square.
See assets/README.txt for exactly what came from where.

A SPRITE STRIP IS READ AS A STRIP

    idle.png  312x52  ->  six 52x52 cels, played in order
    sleep.png  52x52  ->  one cel

A file whose WIDTH IS AN EXACT MULTIPLE OF ITS HEIGHT is that many
frames. That is the whole rule, it matches how every one of these packs
ships, and it means the PNG out of the zip is the PNG that runs -- no
slicing, no generated files, no PIL.

Separate files still work too: idle_1.png, idle_2.png. Numbers sort
properly, so a ten-frame pack does not play 1, 10, 2.

THE PACK'S OWN NAMES ARE ACCEPTED

    Demon_A_Idle.png     ->  idle
    Blood Monster_A_Walk ->  walk

The state is the last word; a trailing _<number> is a frame index. So a
new pack can be dropped in unrenamed and it will mostly just work.

SWAPPING HIM FOR SOMETHING ELSE

    1. Copy the new character's Idle/Walk/Attack01/Hurt strips in as
       idle/walk/happy/sad.png
    2. python3 yuzu_vpet.py     -- says what it found and what is wired
    3. Refresh the page

If the new art is small inside its own frame, crop every state to ONE
shared square box before committing -- per-state crops make him change
size when his mood changes.

RULES THAT MATTER

    - Transparent PNG for the creature. bg is the only solid picture.
    - Same footing across the cels of one state or he bobs about.
    - Pixel art is rendered crisp, not smoothed. Deliberate.
    - Anything called `neutral`, `stand`, `move`, `run`, `cheer`,
      `attack01`, `hurt`, `angry` or `sleepy` is picked up too --
      see ROLES in yuzu_vpet.py.

WHERE MORE COME FROM

    itch.io -> "monster sprites", "creature sprite pack", filter Free,
    check the licence. OpenGameArt.org for rooms and backgrounds.

    Avoid anything that IS an actual Digimon/Pokemon design. Generic
    creature in that spirit only.
