shiro.jpg -- Ghost's picture of Shiro, Sept 26: a small girl in a white
hood and a black gas mask, riding a white six-legged chassis with a
missile pod on each shoulder. 736x982, drawn on white.

NOT CUT OUT, and not meant to be. Her page is the light one and shows
her with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, which makes pure white EXACTLY the
page colour -- the same trick as `screen` on Four's black, the other
way round. Her floor shadow is grey, so it stays on the page as a real
shadow; a cut-out would have thrown it away or left it as a grey blob.

THE ONE EDIT: near-whites pushed to pure white, or they multiply to a
faint grey box. Every channel is left alone up to 220, and 220..240 is
stretched to 220..255, so the backdrop (241..254 in the original) is 255
and her own greys and darks are untouched:

    lut = [v if v <= 220 else min(255, round(220 + (v - 220) * 35 / 20))
           for v in range(256)]
    Image.open(src).convert("RGB").point(lut * 3).save(
        "shiro.jpg", quality=90, subsampling=0, optimize=True)

The original is ui/art_in/shiro_mk2.jpg, so this can be redone.

Her legs run off the left and right edges of the picture, so the page
fades both sides rather than cutting them with a straight line.
Rendered at 1024x600 and 412 and looked at before it shipped.
