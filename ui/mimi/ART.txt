MIMI — where her pictures came from
===================================

Six images, sent by Ghost on Sept 12 2026, named by him in the same
conversation ("move on to mimi"). All six are the SAME character in
different poses, not a costume sheet -- so unlike Yuzu's folder these
are not interchangeable states of one thing and nothing here is
registered to a shared canvas.

  bunny_ghosts      standing, small blue ghosts around her
  crawling          on all fours, blue soul-flames, black backdrop
  ghost_crowd       standing over a crowd of little ghosts
  graveyard         sitting among crosses
  hooded_portrait   bust, hands raised, hood up
  standing_tan      standing, black smoke and two skulls

PROVENANCE IS NOT RECORDED, the same as Cait's picture and for the same
reason: they arrived as finished images and nobody wrote down where
they came from. Saya's faces are Ghost's OWN drawings (ui/raw/ has the
originals off his phone); these are not known to be.

Being straight about it once, in the place it matters: this is a
personal build on his own hardware, which is fine. If the deck is ever
shown off publicly, credit these or swap them.

HOW THEY WERE CUT
-----------------
`python3 yuzu_cutout.py` -- the originals are in ui/art_in/ and the
cuts land in ui/art_out/; these are copies, resized to 720 tall.

He tried an online background remover first and it ate the ghosts:
"i went to remove the background to make it a png online but it removed
the cool parts too like the ghosts behind her." Those services run a
SUBJECT DETECTOR -- they keep the person and drop the rest, so floating
ghosts are background by definition. yuzu_cutout removes the BACKDROP
COLOUR instead and keeps everything that is not it. Every wisp, skull,
flame and cloud survived.

Two of the six needed a recipe, both for the same reason -- her costume
being the backdrop colour -- and both recipes live in
`yuzu_cutout.RECIPES` beside the reason:

  hooded_portrait   white cape and a near-white face on a white page,
                    plus an inset grey panel behind her that a flood
                    from the edge can never reach. Tight tolerance,
                    seven seeds along the panel's margins, trimmed.
  crawling          black gloves and black legs on a black page, with
                    JPEG blocking in the darks.

WHY THEY ARE 720 TALL
---------------------
The panel is 1024x600 and a character renders around 440px tall on it,
so 720 is comfortably above what any screen here shows while keeping
the folder at 2.3MB instead of 3.5MB. Ghost pulls this repo over WiFi
onto a board whose clock breaks TLS on a cold boot; megabytes are not
free. The full-size cuts are in ui/art_out/ if a bigger one is ever
wanted, and the untouched originals are in ui/art_in/.

HER PERSONALITY IS NOT WRITTEN YET, deliberately. Ghost, Sept 12:
"Dont design the characters persona yet im still trying to brainstrorm
her personality." What exists is `personas/_hardware_wisp.txt` -- the
world and the body, no temperament -- and that file says which call is
still his to make.
